# Komaru Cat Mod

A Minecraft mod for **Forge 1.20.1** that adds **Komaru**, an adorable cat companion inspired by the internet-famous feline!

---

## Features

- **Komaru Entity**: A cute, custom-animated cat companion powered by GeckoLib.
  - **Animations**: Custom idle, walk, sprint, sit, lie down, sleeping, and rabbit poses.
  - **Taming**: Tame Komaru using **Komaru Powder**. Once tamed, Komaru will follow and protect you.
  - **Interaction**:
    - Right-click to make Komaru sit or stand.
    - Shift + Right-click to pet and receive heart particles.
    - Right-click with a **Bowl** to toggle owner following mode.
  - **Morning Gifts**: When you sleep in a bed with a tamed Komaru resting nearby, Komaru may bring you a gift in the morning!
  - **Spawning**: Naturally spawns in Birch Forest and Old Growth Birch Forest biomes.
- **Items**:
  - **Komaru Spawn Egg**: Spawn Komaru directly in creative mode or with commands.
  - **Komaru Powder**: Craftable item used to tame and heal Komaru.
- **Custom Paintings**: Includes 8 unique custom paintings featuring Komaru in various iconic poses!

---

## Requirements

- **Minecraft**: `1.20.1`
- **Minecraft Forge**: `47.3.0` or newer
- **GeckoLib**: `4.4.6` or newer
